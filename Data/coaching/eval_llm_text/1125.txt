You did several things in a tour-like way. Your shoulder turn at the top (how much your upper body coiled) was in the tour range, your hip turn at impact (the moment the club meets the ball) was also in range, and your hip shift (side-to-side move) stayed in range too. Your tempo ratio, which is the overall backswing-to-downswing rhythm, was in the tour range as well.

One thing that stood out was your head height at the downswing: your head moved up and down more than is typical in most tour swings. You also turned your hips a lot at the top, which meant the gap between your shoulder turn and hip turn at the top was smaller than tour-pro patterns. There are some solid tour-range pieces in this swing already.
