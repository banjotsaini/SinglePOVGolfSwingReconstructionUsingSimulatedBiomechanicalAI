A few pieces of your motion matched the tour-pro range. At the top, your **x-factor** (the difference between shoulder turn and hip turn) was in range. At impact, your **spine tilt** (how much your upper body is tilted) was also in range. Your **head sway** (side-to-side movement), **head lift** (upward movement), and **hip lateral shift** (small move toward the target) all stayed within the tour range too.

The main observation was your **tempo**: your backswing-to-downswing ratio was longer on the backswing side than the usual tour pattern. In simple terms, your swing used a slower, longer build-up before the club came down.

There are some solid tour-like pieces in this swing, and that is a good base to build from.
