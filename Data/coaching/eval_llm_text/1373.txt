A lot of this swing matched up well with the tour-pro range. At the top, their X-factor, the separation between the shoulders and hips, was in range. Their head lift also stayed in range, which means their height stayed fairly steady, and their hip lateral shift, how much the hips moved sideways, was in range too. Their tempo ratio, the pace of the swing, was also in the tour range.

The main difference showed up in posture at impact, the moment the club meets the ball. Their spine angle changed more from address, the setup position, to impact than you usually see in tour swings, so the body looked more upright by impact. Their head also moved more side to side than is typical in tour swings, drifting farther off the ball.

There are some solid tour-like pieces here, and this gives a good base to build from.
