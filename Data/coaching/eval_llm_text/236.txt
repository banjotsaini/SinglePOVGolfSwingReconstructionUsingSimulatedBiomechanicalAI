Your tempo was also very close to the tour median, and your spine tilt at address (your side bend at setup) and spine tilt at impact were both in range.

A few other pieces also matched the pro pattern well: your posture loss stayed in range, your head lift stayed in range, and your lead knee flex was in range at both address and impact. Altogether, that paints a picture of a swing with several solid, tour-range body motions.

Your hips were less open than the tour-pro range at impact, meaning your pelvis was turned less toward the target at that moment, and your lateral hip shift was also smaller than the tour range, meaning your hips moved less toward the target than pros typically do.
